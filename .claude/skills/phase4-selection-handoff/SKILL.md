# Phase 4: Selection handoff

Begin only after the named decision owner has recorded a final outcome. The agent must not create or alter that outcome.

Prepare a private handoff record containing:

- project identifier;
- candidate identifier;
- decision status and approving role;
- approval timestamp;
- job-related rationale supplied by the decision owner;
- outstanding administrative actions; and
- retention or deletion date for candidate data.

Draft selection and closure communications for human review. Do not include sensitive personal information, interview notes, comparative rankings, or information about other candidates. External messages require explicit approval and must be sent through an authorized private system.
