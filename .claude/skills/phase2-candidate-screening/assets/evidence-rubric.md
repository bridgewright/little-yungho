# Evidence rubric

Use only criteria approved for the role. The following reference criteria illustrate observable evidence and are not universal hiring requirements.

| Criterion | Relevant evidence | Insufficient basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structured research | A work sample separates questions, evidence, assumptions, and conclusions | Institution name or employer prestige alone |
| Source discipline | Claims are traceable to cited, credible sources | Unsupported assertions |
| Written communication | The sample is concise, coherent, and explicit about limitations | Writing style inferred from demographic or linguistic background |
| Quantitative reasoning | Calculations are reproducible and assumptions are stated | A self-reported skill without supporting work |
| Delivery reliability | Availability and required deadlines are explicitly confirmed | Inference from age, location, family status, or other personal context |

Record `meets evidence`, `partial evidence`, or `insufficient evidence` with a source reference. Do not compute an automatic final decision.
