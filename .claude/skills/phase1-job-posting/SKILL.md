# Phase 1: Role publication

Read the approved staffing brief and `assets/job-posting-template.md`. Create one canonical role announcement, then adapt length and formatting for each approved channel without changing requirements or dates.

## Quality checks

- Responsibilities and selection criteria are job related and specific.
- Dates, duration, location expectations, and application instructions agree with the brief.
- The copy does not disclose a confidential client, project code, or employee contact detail.
- The language does not imply automated hiring decisions.
- No post is published automatically.

Present the canonical draft and channel variations together. Ask the human owner to approve or revise each variation. Record only the channel, approved version identifier, and publication status in the private runtime system.
