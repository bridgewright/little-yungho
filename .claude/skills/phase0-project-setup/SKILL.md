# Phase 0: Project setup

Collect the minimum information required to define the role:

- stable project identifier and public-safe title;
- engagement dates and application deadline;
- number of positions;
- responsibilities and job-related selection criteria;
- decision owner role and interview panel roles; and
- approved publication channels.

Do not collect client identities, internal case codes, private email addresses, or candidate information in a public artifact. Represent people by role unless a private operating environment requires otherwise.

Validate that dates are coherent, selection criteria are observable, and the decision owner is named by role. Produce a concise staffing brief and request explicit approval. Do not start publication work until approval is recorded.
