# Responsible use

Hiring is a consequential domain. Little Yungho is designed to support administration and evidence organization, not to replace accountable human judgment.

## Permitted assistance

- Drafting a role description from an approved brief
- Normalizing redacted, job-related evidence
- Applying an explicitly approved rubric
- Identifying missing evidence or scheduling conflicts
- Preparing status summaries and decision records

## Prohibited use

- Fully automated selection, rejection, or candidate communication
- Evaluation based on protected characteristics or proxies for them
- Inference of age, ethnicity, health, family status, socioeconomic status, or other sensitive attributes
- Collection of birth dates, home addresses, photographs, government identifiers, or unrelated personal information
- Fabrication of candidate evidence, interview feedback, or approval

Users are responsible for applicable employment, privacy, discrimination, records-management, and automated-decision laws in their jurisdiction.
